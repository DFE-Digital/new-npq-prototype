Register for a national professional qualification (NPQ)

Use this service to:

  • check if you're eligible for DfE scholarship funding
  • register for an NPQ or the Early Headship Coaching Offer (EHCO)

After registering, your provider will send you an application form to complete.

Before you start

If you are checking your eligibility, you'll need:

  • the NPQ course you want to take and the provider you'll use
  • the address of your workplace
  • an Ofsted unique reference number (URN) if you work in early years or childcare
