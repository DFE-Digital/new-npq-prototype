UR round 2: Setting page iterations & TeacherAuth

March 2026

UR playback deck
UR planning board
Prototype map

In the second round, we tested two versions of the settings page, as well as the TeacherAuth journey.

Users were split between two designs, so there was no clear winner between version 1 and 2. It however uncovered the fundamental issue of multiple options being applicable to some users.

After some investigation, UCD found out that:

  • the settings page was not statistically relevant to any policy team - no essential data is gathered from it
  • the page confused users, no matter how clear the content was to them since they may not perfectly fit the settings binary outlined on the page

Our recommendation was to remove the page, putting the Workplace question first and then deal with the edge cases that don't fit into the path of least resistance which is what we'll be investigating in UR round 3.

This prototype contains content changes made to the eligibility checker that came out of round 2, just to improve comprehension and clarity across the NPQ service.
